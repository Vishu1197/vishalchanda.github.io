What I work on

Three lines of research, one question

Dengue has no licensed antiviral. Every strand of my doctoral work attacks that gap from a different side: find the molecule, understand the disease, then get the molecule to the target.

01

Computational drug discovery

Structure based screening against dengue non structural proteins, hybrid machine learning models for affinity prediction, molecular dynamics to confirm the pose holds, and MM/PBSA for binding free energy. Published as a peer reviewed method in 2025.

02

Dengue transcriptomics

RNA-Seq and microarray analysis of dengue infected tissue to trace how non structural proteins rewire gene regulatory networks and drive thrombocytopenia. Differential expression, pathway enrichment, GSEA and network reconstruction.

03

Nanocarrier delivery

Lipid and polymeric nanoparticles built to carry antiviral phytochemicals past the bioavailability wall that stops most of them. Formulation, characterisation and in vitro antiviral testing against dengue virus serotype 2.
