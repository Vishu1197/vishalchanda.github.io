The background

Doctoral researcher in biotechnology with a practice that runs from a docking grid box to a cell culture plate. The common thread is dengue, and the belief that a computational hit is only worth something once somebody has tried to break it at the bench.

How I work

I combine in silico and in vitro approaches rather than treating them as separate disciplines. A screening campaign that never leaves the computer is a hypothesis, not a result, so every promising candidate in my work is carried through molecular dynamics, binding free energy analysis, ADMET profiling and then an actual antiviral assay.

What I am looking for

A computational biologist role where the modelling is answerable to experiment. Antiviral therapeutics, structure based drug design, translational biotechnology. I am equally open to industry positions and to postdoctoral research, and I expect to complete the doctorate in 2027.
